# Data Dictionary: RFM\_Churn Dataset

## Source

**Database**: AdventureWorks2025  
**Tables used**: Sales.Customer, Sales.SalesOrderHeader  
**Total records**: 19,119 customers  
**Date range**: 2011 — 2025-06-29

## Columns

|Column|Type|Description|
|-|-|-|
|CustomerID|INT|Unique customer identifier|
|recency\_days|INT|Days since last purchase (vs MAX OrderDate)|
|frequency|INT|Count of distinct orders placed|
|monetary|DECIMAL|Total revenue generated by customer|
|r\_score|INT (1-5)|Recency score — 1=most inactive, 5=most recent|
|f\_score|INT (1-5)|Frequency score — 1=fewest orders, 5=most orders|
|m\_score|INT (1-5)|Monetary score — 1=lowest spend, 5=highest spend|
|total\_score|INT (3-15)|Sum of r+f+m scores — lower = higher churn risk|
|churn\_segment|VARCHAR|HIGH RISK / MEDIUM RISK / LOYAL|

## DAX Measures 4 Folders

### Revenue Metrics

|Measure|Description|
|-|-|
|Revenue at Risk|Total revenue from HIGH RISK customers|
|Revenue LOYAL|Total revenue from LOYAL customers|
|Revenue MEDIUM RISK|Total revenue from MEDIUM RISK customers|
|Total Revenue|Sum of all customer revenue|
|% Revenue at Risk|% of total revenue from HIGH RISK (ignores slicers)|
|Revenue Reactivacion|Historical revenue from reactivation targets|
|Revenue Top 100|Revenue from top 100 customers by spend|

### Customer Metrics

|Measure|Description|
|-|-|
|Clientes HIGH RISK|Count of HIGH RISK customers|
|Clientes LOYAL|Count of LOYAL customers|
|Clientes MEDIUM RISK|Count of MEDIUM RISK customers|
|Clientes Total|Total customer count|
|Clientes Reactivacion|LOYAL customers with r\_score = 1|
|% Customers HIGH RISK|Fixed % of HIGH RISK customers (ignores slicers)|

### RFM Analysis

|Measure|Description|
|-|-|
|Avg Recency Days|Average days since last purchase|
|Avg Frequency|Average orders per customer|
|Avg Monetary|Average spend per customer|
|Avg Monetary HIGH RISK|Average spend — HIGH RISK segment|
|Avg Monetary LOYAL|Average spend — LOYAL segment|
|Loyal vs Risk Ratio|How many times more a LOYAL customer spends vs HIGH RISK|
|Risk Score Index|Average total\_score — lower = more overall risk|
